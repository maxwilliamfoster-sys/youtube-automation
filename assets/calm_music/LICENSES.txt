Background music for the BuriedCasefiles documentary pipeline (documentary_composer.py).

All four recordings are PUBLIC DOMAIN on Wikimedia Commons (composition and
recording), so no attribution is legally required and the tracks are free for
commercial use. Each file is the first 140 s of the recording, converted to mono,
gently low-passed (5.5 kHz) and compressed so nothing sounds sharp under the
narration, normalised to about -20 LUFS, with 2 s / 4 s fades. Downloaded 2026-10-12.

If this folder is ever empty, documentary_composer falls back to a quiet ambient pad
generated by FFmpeg, and then to narration only - a run never fails for want of music.
